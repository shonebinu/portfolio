Unlucky me met with an accident last year on **07 April 2025**. It was about noon time and I was on my motor bike to get some uni work done.

An intoxicated car driver who was way overspeed came straight into my lane on a rising curve and hit me head on. I was left unconscious.

Over a dozen blood transfusions, half dozen surgeries in different hospitals, multiple plate and rods(i'm iron man ik /s), skin grafts, nerve grafts, muscle graft and much more. Doctors said I'm lucky to be alive. First time being admitted to a hospital went on for a long time and all family savings are now dry. Thanks to incredible people supporting me, somehow got out of it. Still being treated at multiple hospitals.

The incident happened during my last semester exams and got backlogs which I had to clear by using my non dominant arm to complete my degree after learning to write with it this year. Lost my placements and initial career trajectory.

Therapy continuing still to this day. Functional movements in my right limbs has yet to regain due to nerve ruptures.

Almost half of the day is being spent on therapy including electric stimulation and others, initially cried a lot and I say as a man, a looot. Still to this day, the pain is nothing to scoff at. Some days hated myself, some hated the world, some the offender, some god. I asked "why" a lot, since the fault wasn't mine and why the offender was completely fine. They did not compensate, did not enquire.

Joined a distant masters degree online this year. Since I don't get time for heavy focus sessions, I work on what I'm motivated or intrigued about which usually dry up quickly. Need to get off that habit.

I could write a lot more about this incident, but I don't want to spend more time on thinking about it. I'll turn 22 in a couple of days and I wanted to put this out before that. Signing off.
